🎉 Great news! Oliver has been awarded a two-year postdoctoral fellowship from the Engelhorn Foundation!
<!-- excerpt end -->

With this fellowship, he will investigate how the human brain’s motor networks support movement. Using magnetoencephalography (MEG), his research will explore how these networks evolve over time during behaviour and how they are affected by ageing and disease. Ultimately, the project aims to advance our understanding of motor control and help identify new biomarkers for improved diagnosis and treatment.
 
Congratulations, Oliver! We look forward to sharing the exciting discoveries ahead.
