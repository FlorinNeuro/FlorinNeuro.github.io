We would like to welcome Jule Schwegmann as a new medical doctor candidate to our lab! She has been honored with the award for digital medicine of the German society for digital medicine. 
<!-- excerpt end -->
Her project "Resonance und the Parkinsonian Brain: toward digital decision support systems for personalized neuromodulation" focusses on the digitization of DBS programming in people with PD.
The goal of her project is to develop a tool using a electrophysiological biomarkers from dry-EEG System (DBS evoked potentials) in real time.
