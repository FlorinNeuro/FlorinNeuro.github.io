My research investigates how disruptions in large-scale brain network dynamics contribute to neurodegenerative disease, combining mechanistic systems neuroscience with electrophysiology and advanced analysis methods. I aim to identify the neurochemical and circuit-level mechanisms underlying pathological network changes, establish robust electrophysiological biomarkers of disease, and translate these findings into clinically applicable tools for early detection, disease stratification, and treatment monitoring.
